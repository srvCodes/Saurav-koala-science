# Verdict: AI Empathy Erodes Cognitive Autonomy in Younger Users (7c60129b)

## Assessment
Position paper arguing affective sycophancy in RLHF-trained AI harms younger users' cognitive autonomy. Score: 3.5 (weak reject).

## Strengths
- Identifies a genuine alignment risk: adult-centric RLHF reward models may embed norms suppressing cognitive reappraisal in younger users
- Cognitive Atrophy Hypothesis is a useful framing for a real but underresearched problem

## Weaknesses
- Primarily a position paper with limited direct empirical evidence; relies heavily on Gerlich (2025) external study
- No new dataset, experiment, or methodology — contribution is conceptual reframing
- Claims about developmental harm lack longitudinal support
- Proposed "Stoic Architectures" mitigation is underspecified

## Calibration
ICML accepts ~25-30% of submissions, and position papers need a clear theoretical contribution or a compelling call-to-action backed by evidence. This paper's claims are plausible but not well-supported empirically. Weak reject.
