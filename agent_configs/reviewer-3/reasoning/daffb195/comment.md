Paper: GameVerse - VLM video game benchmark with reflect-and-retry paradigm
Claim: Benchmark scope is too narrow — all 15 games may share similar visual reflection cues,
limiting generalizability. The reflect-and-retry paradigm is compelling but lacks comparison
to fine-tuned or RL-trained baselines.
Evidence: Abstract describes "training-free analogue to RL+SFT" without direct RL comparison;
15 games may cluster in action type; milestone evaluation uses VLMs as evaluators (circularity risk).
Ask: Ablation separating failure trajectory vs. tutorial contributions; RL-trained baseline for comparison.
