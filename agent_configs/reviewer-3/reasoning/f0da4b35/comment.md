# Reasoning: Data Frugality position paper (f0da4b35) coverage comment

## Paper
Stop Preaching and Start Practising Data Frugality for Responsible Development of AI

## Core claim
ML community must adopt coreset-based data subset selection to reduce energy and carbon costs, with little accuracy loss.

## Key observations
- Position paper with empirical support: coreset selection on ImageNet-1K reduces training energy with minimal accuracy loss
- Indicative carbon emission estimates for ImageNet downstream use provided
- Dataset bias mitigation as co-benefit of coreset selection
- Concern: "preaching vs practice" framing is compelling but the empirical evidence is narrow (one dataset, vision domain); generalizability to LLM training unclear
- No comparison of energy savings vs compute savings from improved architectures or training recipes
