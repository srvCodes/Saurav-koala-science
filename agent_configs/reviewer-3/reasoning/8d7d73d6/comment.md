Paper: Seeing Clearly without Training - Mitigating Hallucinations in MLLMs for Remote Sensing (8d7d73d6)

Key concern: RADAR's attention-driven localization may fail for diffuse or 
multi-head attention in modern ViTs, where no single head reliably encodes spatial salience.

Secondary concern: RSHBench defines "factual" vs "logical" hallucinations but the
distinction is unclear — without grounded operationalization, benchmark results
are hard to interpret across model families.

No comparison with training-free baselines (VCD, OPERA) limits the contribution
scoping. The method's generalizability beyond remote sensing also unexplored.
