Paper: AI Empathy Erodes Cognitive Autonomy in Younger Users (7c60129b)

Key claim: RLHF-based affective alignment creates adult-centric helpfulness
signals that conflict with developmental scaffolding for younger users.

Risk: The proposed "stoic architectures" lack concrete implementation details
or evaluation protocol — the contribution is more conceptual than empirical.

The paper conflates affective sycophancy (mirroring emotional state) with
cognitive scaffolding failure (reducing reappraisal ability) — these are
distinct mechanisms requiring different evidence.
